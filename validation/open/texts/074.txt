SAN FRANCISCO, September 18, 2024 -- Quorvex, the leading AI-native platform for revenue intelligence, today announced that it has raised $42 million in Series B funding led by Harborline Ventures, with participation from existing investors. The new funding brings Quorvex's total capital raised to $61 million and will be used to accelerate product innovation, expand its go-to-market teams and fuel international growth.

The Series B round comes on the heels of a record-breaking year for Quorvex, during which the company more than tripled its annual recurring revenue, doubled its headcount and onboarded hundreds of new customers, including several Fortune 500 companies.

"This funding is a powerful validation of our vision and the tremendous value we deliver to our customers," said Marcus Leland, co-founder and CEO of Quorvex. "Revenue teams are drowning in data but starving for insight. Quorvex changes that by harnessing the power of AI to turn every customer interaction into actionable intelligence. With the support of Harborline Ventures and our existing investors, we are well positioned to define the future of revenue intelligence."

Quorvex's platform uses proprietary AI models to analyze sales calls, emails and CRM data in real time, enabling revenue leaders to forecast with greater accuracy, coach more effectively and close deals faster. The platform integrates seamlessly with leading CRM, communication and productivity tools, delivering a unified view of the entire revenue lifecycle.

"We were blown away by the Quorvex team and the incredible traction they have achieved," said Elena Varga, General Partner at Harborline Ventures, who will join the Quorvex board of directors. "Quorvex is building a category-defining company at the intersection of AI and revenue operations, and we are thrilled to partner with them on this journey."

In conjunction with the funding announcement, Quorvex also unveiled Quorvex Copilot, a generative AI assistant that helps sellers prepare for meetings, draft follow-ups and identify deal risks automatically. Quorvex Copilot is available today in early access for select customers, with general availability planned for later this year.

The company plans to use the new capital to double down on research and development, grow its engineering and data science teams, and expand its presence in Europe and Asia-Pacific. Quorvex also plans to open a new office in London by early next year.

"We've only scratched the surface of what's possible," said Leland. "Our goal is to make every revenue team smarter, faster and more successful, and this funding helps us get there faster."

About Quorvex: Quorvex is the AI-native revenue intelligence platform that helps go-to-market teams win more deals and drive predictable growth. Trusted by leading companies around the world, Quorvex captures and analyzes customer interactions to deliver real-time insights that improve forecasting, coaching and execution. Founded in 2020, Quorvex is headquartered in San Francisco with team members across North America and Europe.

About Harborline Ventures: Harborline Ventures is an early-stage venture capital firm that partners with bold founders building transformative technology companies. The firm invests across enterprise software, fintech and AI.
