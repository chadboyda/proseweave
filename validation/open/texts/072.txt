AUSTIN, Texas, March 12, 2024 -- Veltrana Systems, a leading provider of innovative cloud-based workforce solutions, today announced the launch of Veltrana Pulse, a next-generation, AI-powered employee engagement platform designed to help organizations of all sizes unlock the full potential of their people.

Veltrana Pulse leverages advanced artificial intelligence and machine learning to deliver real-time, actionable insights into employee sentiment, productivity and wellbeing. The platform seamlessly integrates with existing HR systems, enabling organizations to drive meaningful engagement, reduce turnover and foster a culture of continuous improvement.

"In today's rapidly evolving workplace, organizations need more than just data -- they need insights they can act on," said Jordan Kessler, Chief Executive Officer of Veltrana Systems. "Veltrana Pulse represents a major milestone in our mission to empower organizations to build happier, more productive teams. We are incredibly excited to bring this game-changing solution to market and we look forward to helping our customers transform the way they engage their workforce."

Key features of Veltrana Pulse include AI-driven sentiment analysis, customizable pulse surveys, predictive attrition modeling, intuitive dashboards and enterprise-grade security. The platform is designed to scale effortlessly from small businesses to global enterprises, delivering a best-in-class experience for HR leaders, managers and employees alike.

Early adopters of Veltrana Pulse have reported significant improvements in engagement scores and retention rates. "Since implementing Veltrana Pulse, we've gained unprecedented visibility into how our people are feeling," said Dana Morrow, Vice President of People Operations at a Fortune 1000 manufacturing company. "The insights have been truly transformational for our organization."

The launch of Veltrana Pulse builds on Veltrana Systems' strong momentum. In the past year, the company has expanded its customer base, grown its team and been recognized with multiple industry awards for innovation and customer satisfaction.

"Our customers are at the heart of everything we do," added Kessler. "With Veltrana Pulse, we are doubling down on our commitment to delivering innovative solutions that create real value for our customers and their employees."

Veltrana Pulse is available immediately to new and existing customers. To learn more or to request a demo, visit the Veltrana Systems website.
