CHICAGO, June 4, 2024 -- Northmere Foods, a trusted name in better-for-you snacking, is thrilled to announce a strategic partnership with Glenharrow Markets, one of the fastest-growing regional grocery chains in the Midwest, to bring its award-winning line of plant-based snack bars to more than 200 Glenharrow Markets locations beginning this summer.

The partnership marks an exciting new chapter for Northmere Foods as the company continues to expand its retail footprint and deliver delicious, nutritious snacking options to health-conscious consumers across the country.

"We are extremely proud to partner with Glenharrow Markets, a retailer that truly shares our values and our passion for quality," said Priya Anand, Founder and CEO of Northmere Foods. "This partnership is a testament to the incredible demand we are seeing for our products and the hard work of our amazing team. We can't wait for Glenharrow shoppers to experience the Northmere difference."

Northmere Foods' snack bars are made with simple, wholesome ingredients and are free from artificial flavors, colors and preservatives. The line includes a variety of crave-worthy flavors, including Dark Chocolate Sea Salt, Peanut Butter Crunch, Wild Blueberry and the brand-new Maple Pecan, which will debut exclusively at Glenharrow Markets.

"At Glenharrow Markets, we are always looking for innovative brands that resonate with our customers," said Tom Brenner, Vice President of Merchandising at Glenharrow Markets. "Northmere Foods is a perfect fit for our growing better-for-you category, and we are excited to offer our shoppers these high-quality, great-tasting products."

To celebrate the launch, Northmere Foods will host in-store sampling events at select Glenharrow Markets locations throughout the month of July. Shoppers can also take advantage of special introductory pricing for a limited time.

The Glenharrow partnership is the latest milestone in a year of remarkable growth for Northmere Foods. The company recently expanded its manufacturing capacity, launched a refreshed brand identity and welcomed several key hires to its leadership team. Northmere Foods products are now available in thousands of retail locations nationwide, as well as online.

"This is just the beginning," Anand added. "We have big plans for the rest of the year and we look forward to sharing more exciting news very soon."

About Northmere Foods: Founded in 2018, Northmere Foods is on a mission to make healthy snacking simple, delicious and accessible for everyone. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, the company creates plant-based snacks made with real, wholesome ingredients. Northmere Foods products are available at leading retailers nationwide and online. For more information, visit the Northmere Foods website and follow the brand on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

About Glenharrow Markets: Glenharrow Markets is a family-owned grocery chain dedicated to providing fresh, high-quality products and exceptional customer service to the communities it serves. With more than 200 locations across the Midwest, Glenharrow Markets is proud to be a neighborhood destination for families.
