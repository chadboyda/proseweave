You won't believe what happened when this waitress found a note under a customer's plate. The ending will restore your faith in humanity.

It was a normal Tuesday night at a small diner in a quiet town. Jenny, a 24-year-old single mom, was working a double shift like she did most days. She had been on her feet for almost twelve hours and her feet were killing her. But she kept smiling, because that's just who she is.

Around 9pm an older man came in and sat down at a booth in the corner. He was wearing a worn-out jacket and he looked tired. He ordered a cup of coffee and a slice of apple pie. Nothing special. Jenny brought him his food and asked him how his day was going.

What happened next was something nobody expected.

The man started talking. He told Jenny that his wife had passed away six months ago and that this diner was where they used to come every Tuesday night for forty years. He said he hadn't been back since she died, because it was too hard. But tonight he decided to come back one last time.

Jenny sat down with him for a few minutes, even though she was busy. She listened to his stories about his wife. She laughed at his jokes. She told him about her little boy at home. They talked for almost twenty minutes.

When the man left, Jenny went to clean his table. That's when she noticed something under his plate.

It was a folded napkin with a note written on it.

Jenny couldn't believe what she was reading.

The note said: "Thank you for listening. You reminded me of my wife tonight. She always had time for people, too. Please take care of that little boy of yours." Underneath the note was a tip that Jenny will never forget.

It was enough money to cover her rent for three months.

Jenny broke down in tears right there in the diner. Her coworkers came over to see what was wrong, and when they read the note, they started crying too.

Jenny later posted a photo of the note online and it quickly went viral. Thousands of people shared it and left comments about how much it touched them. Many people said it was exactly what they needed to see today.

But that's not even the best part.

A few weeks later, the man came back to the diner. And this time, he wasn't alone.

He brought his grandson, who he said he hadn't seen in years. He told Jenny that talking to her had inspired him to reach out to his family again. Now they come to the diner together every Tuesday night.

Sometimes the smallest act of kindness can change someone's life. You never know what someone is going through.
