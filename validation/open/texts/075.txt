DENVER, January 9, 2024 -- Orbisel Health, an industry-leading provider of patient engagement technology, is pleased to announce the appointment of Karen Whitfield as Chief Operating Officer, effective immediately. In her new role, Whitfield will oversee Orbisel Health's day-to-day operations and play a key role in driving the company's next phase of growth.

Whitfield brings more than 20 years of experience in healthcare technology, operations and strategic leadership to Orbisel Health. Most recently, she served as Senior Vice President of Operations at a national healthcare services organization, where she led large, cross-functional teams and spearheaded numerous initiatives to improve efficiency, quality and customer satisfaction.

"We are delighted to welcome Karen to the Orbisel Health leadership team," said Steven Park, President and Chief Executive Officer of Orbisel Health. "Karen is a proven leader with a strong track record of operational excellence and a deep passion for improving the patient experience. Her expertise will be invaluable as we continue to scale our business and deliver innovative solutions to our customers."

As Chief Operating Officer, Whitfield will be responsible for overseeing customer success, implementation, support and business operations. She will work closely with the executive team to align operational strategy with the company's long-term vision and ensure that Orbisel Health continues to provide best-in-class service to its growing base of healthcare provider customers.

"I am honored and excited to join Orbisel Health at such a pivotal time in the company's journey," said Whitfield. "Orbisel Health has built an incredible platform and an amazing team, and I look forward to working together to help healthcare organizations deliver better, more connected experiences for their patients."

The appointment of Whitfield follows a period of significant growth for Orbisel Health. Over the past year, the company has expanded its customer base, launched several new product features and strengthened its leadership team with a series of strategic hires. Orbisel Health's platform now supports millions of patient interactions every month.

Whitfield holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration and a Master of Business Administration. She is an active member of several professional healthcare organizations and has been a featured speaker at numerous industry conferences.

"Karen's appointment reflects our ongoing commitment to building a world-class organization," Park added. "We are confident that her leadership will help us achieve our ambitious goals and continue to make a meaningful difference in healthcare."

About Orbisel Health: Orbisel Health is a leading provider of patient engagement technology that empowers healthcare organizations to communicate with patients more effectively. The company's cloud-based platform offers appointment reminders, digital intake, two-way messaging and patient feedback tools that help providers improve outcomes, reduce no-shows and enhance the patient experience. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado, Orbisel Health serves hundreds of healthcare organizations across the United States. For more information, visit the Orbisel Health website.
